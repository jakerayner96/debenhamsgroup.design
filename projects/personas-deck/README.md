# Meet our customers: deck

**Live:** jakerayner96.github.io/debenhamsgroup.design/projects/personas-deck/ · 48 slides
**Source:** "Meet our customers" PDF (`assets/ds/personas/meet-our-customers.pdf`, 9 Oct 2026). Every word on a slide comes from it, through `assets/ds/personas-data.js`. Slides are built from that data, so the deck, the DS Persona cards (#personas) and the persona tests can't drift apart.
**Format:** the AI Generated Badge deck (jakerayner96.github.io/ai-generated-badge): fixed 1920×1080 stage scaled to the window, Antonio caps + Geologica, 96px grid texture, glow, ghost numerals, rise reveals. Borrowed from the Brand Room deck: the site switch in the HUD and the G overview. Site colours are the DS fascia tokens (PLT and boohooMAN take their light tone on black).

## Slides
1. Cover: Meet our customers, with the 40 portraits drifting behind
2. The PDF intro, the four figures (count up), the five sites, the season key
3. Per site (×5): divider with the logo, the Full personas and scenarios link, and the eight headline figures as bars (click a portrait to open that persona)
4. Per persona (×40): portrait, tag, name and role, headline figure + meter, attribute chips, description, the eight scenarios with season dots (the key filters them), the finding + status, and the site's eight personas at top right to jump between them
5. Close: all 40 by site + the PDF footnote

## Keys
← → / space / click · **G** overview · **1–5** jump to a site · Home / End · `#n` deep links · print to PDF = one slide per page.
