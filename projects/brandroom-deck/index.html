01  —  The Brand Room

A destination
of its own

Over the past two years, The Brand Room has delivered strong, sustained double-digit growth and significant margin gains.

With that momentum, The Brand Room becomes a standalone destination, independent from the Debenhams platform. A premium, curated environment that gives our partner brands the prominence they deserve.

Why

Why a standalone
Brand Room

01
Elevated brand positioning

A dedicated environment lets us present partner brands with the premium treatment and storytelling they expect.

02
Improved customer journey

The Brand Room was hard to find inside the Debenhams ecosystem. A separate site gives immediate visibility and a cleaner path to purchase.

03
Stronger curation

Marketplace dilution and legacy platform limits made the edit hard to control. The new site gives full control over curation, discovery and the on-site experience.

04
Clearer identity

Leaving the department-store context behind removes its perception barriers and gives The Brand Room a modern, aspirational identity.
