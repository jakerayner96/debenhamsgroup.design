# 23 · Gift card email redesign (16 Sep 2026)

Own repo: **github.com/jakerayner96/debenhams-giftcard-email** · local `Desktop/PROTOTYPES/giftcard-email` · live **jakerayner96.github.io/debenhams-giftcard-email**.

Brief (Jake, 16 Sep): redesign the transactional "You've been sent a gift card!" email (Mailgun, `giftcard@orders.emaildebenhams.com`, template "GIFT CARD SEND V2") — sleek and modern, design-system rules where possible, new repo. Source material: the 10 Nov 2025 email to Jake (£40.25 refund) and the 8 Sep 2026 Adam Kerr refund case (£4.16) forwarded to Demi Adesanya.

## What shipped
- `template/giftcard.html` — 600px table email on Debenhams-mode tokens (hard-coded hex, token map in the head comment): Geologica 300/600, Primary Aqua card with black ink, DS buttons (50px, 16/24 SemiBold, 4px; secondary white + #70BEB3 outline), Grey 5 body floor, panels 8px, card 16px (radius-16 primitive, hero object only). Mailgun handlebars: `{{#if is_refund}}…{{else}}…{{/if}}` switches refund copy (headline "Your £40.25 refund, ready to spend" + Order / Refund method / Valid until list) vs gift copy ("A gift for you" + sender's message in a Neutral panel). Number + PIN in their own panel with a save-to-account link; "How to redeem" in three steps; fascias as a quiet Grey 5 logo row above the footer instead of the black mid-card strip.
- `index.html` — dark preview shell: New vs Original, Refund vs Gift, 375/600/760, editable merge fields, copy rendered HTML.
- `assets/logos/*.png` — 2× renders of the DS brand SVGs via headless Chrome (strip logos pre-tinted #6B6B6B). Referenced by absolute Pages URL so the template works pasted into an ESP.
- `original/giftcard-original.html` — the production email from the `.eml`, card number / PIN / recipient redacted.

## Decisions / assumptions
- Debenhams mode only; the card is shared across the group so no per-fascia variants yet.
- Light only (`color-scheme: light`); Outlook drops rounded corners; Geologica falls back to Arial in Gmail/Outlook.
- Copy is new (old template said "A gift for you!" to refund customers). Expiry surfaced on the card and in the fact list.
