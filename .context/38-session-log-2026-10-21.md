# 38 · Session log — 20–21 Oct 2026 · App Store Screenshot Exporter

New tool: `tools/app-store-screens/` (page) + `tools/app-store-screens/runner/` (local Node runner). The full spec is in its README. It's linked from Planning and Tools (`DG_TOOLS` in index.html).

## Why
The app store screenshots for every brand and language were done by hand. Jake's brief (21 Oct) was for the tech team:
1. The dev posts the Expo links in Slack.
2. Jake pastes them in.
3. The brand is detected, and the screens come from the brand team's own screenshots.
4. The tool exports every App Store and Play size in every language into Figma-ready folders.

## State
- The page is static on Pages and talks to `localhost:4567`. CORS allows `jakerayner96.github.io` and localhost, and sends the `Access-Control-Allow-Private-Network` header. Use Chrome.
- The runner uses Maestro for UI driving, simctl/adb for screenshots and the status bar, and bundletool for AABs. `learn.js` is the Claude vision route-learner (Opus 5.5, `fallbacks: "default"`).

## Gotchas worth remembering
- **Only one simulator/emulator at a time** on a 16 GB Mac. Three at once crashed Jake's laptop on 20 Oct.
- **iOS `simctl keychain reset`** is the only reliable sign-out. Clearing app state keeps the login, and the app has no sign-out button.
- **iPad "Save Password?"** is a system sheet outside the app hierarchy. Dismiss it by position (43%,57%) on iPad only.
- **iPadOS 26 windowed mode** draws a resize handle in the corner. Fix: Settings → Multitasking & Gestures → Full-Screen Apps.
- **Play screenshots must be at most 2:1**, so the 20:9 phone AVDs don't qualify. Use a custom 1080×1920 AVD.
- **Emulator DNS fails behind a VPN** (21 Oct resolver 172.31.31.123). Boot with `-dns-server 8.8.8.8,1.1.1.1`.
- **Android 13+ asks for notification permission** over the first screen. `pm grant <pkg> android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS` before launch.
- **Maestro `tapOn: point`** takes whole-number percentages only. `"96.7%"` throws NumberFormatException.
- **Signed-in-only data:** the boohooMAN wishlist needs sign-in, but the guest bag doesn't. Signing in merges the guest bag into the account bag, and the account shows staff codes and Premier "Active". So guest screens are taken first, then sign-in.
