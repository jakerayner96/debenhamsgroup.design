Start the runner on your Mac

This page drives the iPhone/iPad Simulator and Android emulator on your Mac through a small local runner. Install it once (needs Xcode, Android Studio and Node.js); it then starts by itself at login. Use Chrome.

curl -fsSL https://jakerayner96.github.io/debenhamsgroup.design/tools/app-store-screens/runner/install.sh | bash
Already installed? Chrome asks once to let this page access other apps and services on this device — click Allow, then check again. Or open the tool straight from your Mac:localhost:4567

Installed but stopped? launchctl load ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.debenhamsgroup.appstorescreens.plist

Choose the screens

Pick the pages from the list, or upload the brand's screenshots and the tool works out which page each one is. At least 5 screens; add as many more as you need. They export in this order.

SKUs open the exact product in every country; product names are searched in the app (English stores). Ticking a page adds it to the list below. Need two of the same (e.g. two product pages)? Use Add another screen.

Test account

For the screens that need signing in (Wishlist, My account). Stored in this Mac's Keychain, never on the site.

    Update the runner
    Run the install command again to pick up the latest version.
    curl -fsSL https://jakerayner96.github.io/debenhamsgroup.design/tools/app-store-screens/runner/install.sh | bash
        
    How the brands ask for screens
    
        
    Either they list the pages (Home, a category, a product SKU…) and you tick them under Pick from the app, or they screenshot the live app, one image per screen, numbered in order, and you drop them into Upload reference screens. Each upload is read on this Mac and matched to its page; add the SKU or category URL it needs. After that, every new build is one paste and one Export.
