App Store Screenshot Brief

Ecom team → creative team. Fill in every field and add one reference for each screen.
At the end you’ll get a PDF to send to creative, who will capture exactly what you reference.

About the brief

Listing type
Sets needed

Screens

In order: screens 1–3 matter most. For every screen give ONE reference: a screenshot from the previous build showing exactly how it should look, OR a link to the equivalent page on the website with the scroll position and any filters or selections. Max one brand panel per set, never in positions 1–3.

Before you send

Tick each one. Anything left unticked shows on the PDF.

Review & export

Export the PDF and send it to creative. Gaps are flagged in red on the PDF, so you can export at any point.
